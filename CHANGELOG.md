## 0.1.2

### English

Commerce qualification certificates can now grant their bound holder permission to plant all tobacco varieties outside their usual biomes. Configure the privilege under **New certificate → Cigarettes: Embers**. The server checks the live certificate record: expiry and revocation remove the privilege; planted crops are unaffected. Soil requirements remain in force.

Updated project metadata, logo, author and homepage. Chinese and English text are provided for the new controls and messages.

Requires Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10 and Java 17. Update both server and clients to 0.1.2. Commerce and JEI remain optional. Back up worlds before updating.

For fictional gameplay only. Smoking harms health; do not imitate in real life.

### 中文

新增商业模组种植资格证联动。在 **新增资格证 → 香烟:余烬** 中选择取消烟草群系限制，绑定玩家即可在有效期内跨群系种植各花色烟草。服务端核对资格证实际记录；过期或吊销后恢复限制，已经种植的作物不受影响，原有土壤要求保留。

更新项目标志、作者、官网和模组资料；新界面与提示均提供中英文。

适用于 Minecraft 1.20.1、Forge 47.4.10、Java 17。服务端和客户端同时更新至 0.1.2，商业模组与 JEI 仍为可选联动。升级前请备份世界。

仅供虚构游戏娱乐，现实请勿模仿。吸烟有害健康。
