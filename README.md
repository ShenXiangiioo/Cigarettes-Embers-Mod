# Cigarettes: Embers

**Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · Singleplayer & multiplayer**

Plant tobacco, hang the leaves to dry, and blend them into fictional cigarette collections. Open a pack, pass one to a friend, or lend a flame. Packs, lighters and ashtrays have their own models and animations, with burning tips, falling ash and smoke that follows movement.

**For fictional gameplay and entertainment only. Do not imitate these actions in real life. Smoking harms health. This mod does not promote real tobacco products.**

## From seed to pack

Break wild grass in survival mode for a 10% chance of finding a tobacco seed. Of those drops, red, yellow, green and purple flowers have a 40:30:20:10 distribution. Each variety grows in its own regions; purple tobacco is the rarest.

Harvest the leaves and hang them on drying racks. Drying takes 20 minutes, or 15 in dry regions. Rain wets the leaves. Removing an unfinished leaf preserves half its current drying progress.

The processor takes tobacco and additives in four shared ingredient slots, in any order. Supply paper and fuel, choose a matching recipe, and start the machine. Inputs stay locked while it works. Stop returns the unfinished batch's ingredients; spent fuel is not refunded. Install **JEI** to look up processing and drying recipes with **R**.

## Small details, shared moments

Different packs use different opening motions. Classic cigarettes are thicker, slim varieties are finer, and the Archon collection has longer, color-matched cigarettes. The cigarette shortens as it burns; the ember brightens during a drag.

Lighters have distinct flames and handling animations. Hold one alight to illuminate the surroundings; every ten seconds consumes one use. Each lighter holds 90 uses. Three ashtray styles include glass and brass, and a freshly extinguished butt briefly smolders.

| Action | Default control |
| :--- | :--- |
| Open a pack / prepare a cigarette | Right-click with a pack or cigarette |
| Light a cigarette | Hold the action key with a fueled lighter available |
| Take a drag | Empty hand, hold right-click; release to exhale |
| Offer a cigarette | Sneak + right-click with a pack, facing another player |
| Accept an offered cigarette | Face the giver and right-click with an empty hand |
| Lend a flame | Sneak + right-click with a lighter, facing the recipient |
| Extinguish into an ashtray | Right-click the ashtray |
| Cancel an action | Right-click again or press Esc |

Controls can be changed in Minecraft's key settings. Other usable blocks keep their normal interactions.

## A few things to discover

Some collections carry game-only effects: Archon helps against undead and the Ender Dragon, Tianxia grants brief flight, Northland can yield extra apples from natural leaves, and Huazhang restores food. These effects require **six cumulative seconds of effective inhaling on the same cigarette**, and obey their own cooldowns and server settings.

Try offering a cigarette to a villager, zombie, skeleton, creeper or zombie villager, then lighting it. Smoking mobs pause their wandering; some hostile mobs temporarily leave the giver alone. A lit villager gives the giver a temporary trade discount. These effects do not grant every player permanent protection or discounts.

The first join and first smoke each have an advancement. Excessive use also has consequences: the sixteenth effectively smoked cigarette in one game day triggers an hour of online addiction time. During addiction, ordinary food cannot restore hunger or saturation; vanilla rotten flesh remains usable. This restriction also covers compatible modded foods.

## Optional Commerce integration

The mod works on its own. With **Commerce** installed, administrators can use its tablet to configure stress gains and relief, disable stress completely, and separately control brand effects, addiction, mob gifts and villager discounts. Disabling stress also removes its meter, scoring and stress penalties.

Use an administrator tablet on a refill station to set its fee. A paid refill debits the player's Commerce account, credits the treasury and records the expense in the phone's transaction history. Paid stations do not require a lava bucket; a fully fueled lighter appears only after a successful payment. Free stations keep the material-based refill.

**New in 0.1.2 — cultivation certificates.** In the qualification dispenser's **New certificate → Cigarettes: Embers** page, enable the tobacco biome waiver. The issued certificate lets its bound player plant all tobacco varieties across biomes during its validity. Expiry or revocation restores the restrictions immediately; existing crops keep growing. Soil requirements still apply. Validity follows Commerce's real-day certificate rules.

## Install

Use **Minecraft Java 1.20.1**, **Forge 47.4.10** and **Java 17**. Put the mod JAR in the `mods` folder. Multiplayer servers and all joining clients need the same version. Keep only one version enabled and back up worlds before updating. **Commerce and JEI are optional.** This is a Forge mod, not a Fabric or Bedrock add-on.

# 香烟：余烬

**Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · 单人和多人游戏**

从种下烟草、晾晒叶片，到调配并加工成一盒虚构品牌的香烟。拆开烟盒，给朋友递上一支，或替对方借个火。烟盒、火机和烟灰缸各有模型与动作，燃烧的烟头、飘落的烟灰和随运动变化的烟雾也都能在游戏里看到。

**本模组仅供虚构游戏娱乐，请勿在现实生活中模仿。吸烟有害健康，未成年人请勿吸烟。本模组不推广现实烟草产品。**

## 从种子到烟盒

生存模式打掉野草，有 10% 概率掉落一粒烟草种子；在掉落的种子中，红花、黄花、绿花、紫花的比例为 40:30:20:10。各品种适合不同地区，紫花最稀有。

收获叶片后挂到晾晒杆上，通常需要 20 分钟，干燥地区需要 15 分钟。雨水会让叶片回潮；提前取下时保留当前进度的一半，再挂上可以接着晒。

加工机的四个原料格共用，烟草与配料顺序不限。放入纸和燃料，原料符合配方后即可开工。加工时原料锁定；点击停机可退回未完成批次的配料，已消耗的燃料不退。安装可选的 **JEI** 后，按 **R** 可以查询加工与晾晒配方。

## 手边的小动作

不同烟盒有不同的开合方式。经典款更粗，细支更纤细，执政官系列有更长且与包装配色对应的香烟。烟会随着燃烧逐渐变短，吸气时烟头更亮。

火机有不同的火焰和把玩动作。长按点燃可以照亮周围，每持续燃烧十秒消耗一次使用次数，每只火机最多 90 次。烟灰缸有三种样式，包括玻璃与古铜；刚灭进去的烟蒂会短暂冒烟。

| 操作 | 默认按键 |
| :--- | :--- |
| 拆盒、取烟或准备单支香烟 | 手持烟盒或香烟右键 |
| 点烟 | 有可用火机时长按动作键 |
| 吸烟 | 空手长按右键，松开吐烟 |
| 递烟 | 手持烟盒，面向对方潜行右键 |
| 接烟 | 面向递烟者，空手右键 |
| 借火 | 手持火机，面向对方潜行右键 |
| 在烟灰缸灭烟 | 右键烟灰缸 |
| 取消动作 | 再次右键或按 Esc |

可以在游戏按键设置中修改操作键。箱子、椅子等可交互方块仍保留原本的右键操作。

## 可以试着发现的彩蛋

部分系列带有纯游戏效果：执政官对不死生物与末影龙有额外伤害，天下提供短时飞行，北境能让天然树叶额外掉落苹果，华章可恢复饱食度。需要对**同一支烟累计有效吸气六秒**才会触发，并受冷却时间和服务器设置控制。

试着给村民、僵尸、骷髅、苦力怕或僵尸村民递烟，再替它点燃。叼烟的生物会停下闲逛，一些敌对生物会暂时不再攻击递烟者；点燃村民嘴里的烟，还能获得临时交易优惠。这些效果只对相应递烟者生效，不会让全服永久和平或永久打折。

第一次进入游戏、第一次吸烟，各有一项成就。过量使用也有代价：一个游戏日内有效吸食第十六支烟，会进入一小时在线计时的烟瘾状态。期间普通食物不能回复饱食度和饱和度，原版腐肉仍可使用；兼容的其他模组食品也受此规则影响。

## 与商业模组联动

不安装商业模组也能独立游玩。安装 **Commerce** 后，管理员可通过后台平板调整压力增加与减少条件，彻底关闭压力系统，或分别控制品牌效果、烟瘾、生物递烟和村民优惠。关闭压力后，进度条、后台积分和压力负面效果都会停止。

管理员手持平板右键充气站，可以设置充气费。收费模式从玩家商业账户扣款，资金进入国库，手机流水同步记录；不再需要岩浆桶，付款成功后才产出满燃料火机。免费模式仍按原有材料规则充气。

**0.1.2 新增种植资格证。** 在资格证发放器的 **新增资格证 → 香烟:余烬** 中，勾选取消烟草群系限制。发证后，绑定玩家可在证件有效期内跨群系种植所有花色烟草。过期或吊销立即恢复限制，已种植的作物继续生长；土壤要求仍然保留。有效期沿用商业模组按现实天数计算的规则。

## 安装

使用 **Minecraft Java 1.20.1、Forge 47.4.10、Java 17**，将模组 JAR 放入 `mods` 文件夹。多人游戏的服务端和所有客户端需安装相同版本，每端只保留一个启用版本；更新前先备份世界。**商业模组和 JEI 都是可选联动。** 本模组适用于 Forge，不适用于 Fabric 或基岩版。
